# ySupport privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

This policy covers ySupport, an AI-assisted support bot in the official Yearn
Discord.

## Information ySupport processes

- Messages and attachments in designated support tickets.
- Discord user, message, channel, and server IDs needed to run support.
- Wallet addresses, transaction hashes, links, errors, and other details you
  choose to share.
- Public messages that authorized contributors bring into support with a
  ySupport trigger.
- Closed ticket transcripts that an authorized maintainer selects for manual review.

The bot limits processing to those support workflows.

## How ySupport uses information

- Investigate requests, answer questions, and keep context across replies.
- Consult documentation, source code, and on-chain information.
- Send issues that need human help to authorized support contributors.
- Operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.
- Review selected cases to find recurring documentation or support gaps.

These purposes exclude sale, advertising, and advertising profiles. ySupport
uses OpenAI services, including Codex, to generate or verify replies from
selected support text and attachments. ySupport does not train its own model
on Discord messages. OpenAI's terms and account data controls apply to the
content sent there.

Discord carries the conversation. For human handoffs, Telegram can carry a
limited summary and relevant evidence to an authorized team channel. Hosting
and storage providers process data to operate the service. Access to stored
support data is limited to authorized operators and contributors who need it.

## How long ySupport keeps information

| Information | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Ticket state | The bot removes its ticket state when Discord deletes the ticket channel. |
| Active public conversation | Context expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. |
| Pending public handoff | The bot retains separate context until delivery, dismissal, or confirmed deletion of the source. It keeps failed deliveries for team review. |
| Resumable AI session | Expires after seven days by default. The bot removes expired sessions during maintenance. |
| Per-turn workspace | The bot deletes temporary files, including downloaded attachments, after the turn. |
| Diagnostic exports | Disabled by default. Operators who enable them limit and delete the exported records. |

## Your choices and requests

Select a ySupport category to opt in to automated replies. Select **Stop Bot**
to stop them. An authorized contributor can still invoke one reply. The bot
handles requests to delete operational records separately.

For questions about privacy, access, or deletion, open a support ticket in the
official Yearn Discord. Say that the request concerns ySupport and include the
Discord user and ticket channel IDs if you have them. Applicable security,
legal, and operational requirements govern these requests.
